Our Story

A sanctuary of warmth
in the heart of the Emirates

Born from a passion for exceptional coffee and genuine hospitality, Mayar Café is a place where moments become memories.

More than coffee —
a feeling

Nestled in the peaceful Al Salmah district of Umm Al Quwain, Mayar Café was crafted to be a refuge from the rush — a place where the aroma of fresh-brewed coffee mingles with warmth, conversation, and care.

From our signature Eggs Benedict to our meticulously crafted lattes and matchas, every item on our menu is prepared with intention. Our team — the heart of Mayar — greets every guest with a smile that feels like coming home.
